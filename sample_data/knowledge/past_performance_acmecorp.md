# Past Performance: Telco Call Centre Transformation (AcmeCorp)

In 2024, we delivered AI-powered quality assurance for a 2,400-agent call centre. Our platform scored 100% of voice interactions and 95% of digital chat interactions automatically. Sentiment mapping across all channels identified a 12% uplift in CSAT within one quarter. The solution integrated with Salesforce and Cisco Finesse via REST APIs.

**Key metrics from that project:**
- 100% interaction ingestion for voice
- 95% automated scoring coverage for digital
- 12% CSAT improvement in 90 days
- 99.9% platform uptime observed over 12 months

**Technical details:** The QA engine used domain-tuned transcription and a rules-based scoring model, with a manager dashboard for calibration. Coaching loops were delivered via an asynchronous training module integrated with the LMS.
