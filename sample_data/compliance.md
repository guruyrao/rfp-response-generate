# Compliance Rules for RFP Responses

1. **Grounding**: All capability claims, metrics, and past-project references MUST be grounded in the company knowledge base or the organization template. Do not invent capabilities.
2. **Forbidden terms**: Never use "guarantee 100%", "no downtime ever", or "free of charge" unless the knowledge base proves them.
3. **Mandatory requirements**: Every criterion marked priority `critical` or `high` in the requirements document MUST have a direct response paragraph.
4. **Template adherence**: The response MUST follow the structure of `templates/response_template.md`.
5. **Completeness**: No section may be left as a placeholder or "TBD".
6. **Accuracy**: Response text must not contradict retrieved knowledge.
